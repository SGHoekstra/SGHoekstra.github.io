Teaching

I developed and taught a lecture series for the MSc Econometrics course Behavioural Macro and Finance together with Cars Hommes at the University of Amsterdam. I also give an annual guest lecture at the Tinbergen Institute, supervise BSc Econometrics theses and have tutored undergraduate microeconomics and linear algebra. I like to give students working code alongside the theory, so that models become something they can run, break and extend.

Behavioural Macro and Finance

MSc, University of Amsterdam  ·  Lecturer  ·  2025 – present

A computational lecture series on agent-based macroeconomics. Students work through a sequence of Pluto notebooks in Julia, built on BeforeIT.jl, and move from running a pre-calibrated model of a whole economy to extending it and estimating its behavioural parameters. Read the course online for the tutorials with explanations, figures and code, and both lecture decks. The source files are on GitHub.

Notebooks

  • 00 — Julia and Pluto primer
    An optional introduction to Julia syntax and Pluto's reactive notebooks.
  • 01 — Meet the model
    Run the Austrian model, explore its agents and production network, and check stock-flow consistency.
  • 02 — Calibration and forecasting
    National-accounts calibration, initial conditions, Monte Carlo fan charts and an AR(1) forecast benchmark.
  • 03 — Shocks, expectations and policy
    Shock processes, bankruptcy cascades, expectation formation and the Taylor rule.
  • 04 — Extending the model: CANVAS
    Add a wage–price spiral and trace its pass-through channels.
  • 05 — Likelihood-free calibration
    An online overview of ABC, NPE and SNRE. The full notebook runs locally with Julia and Python.
  • Cheatsheet
    A quick reference to keep open in a second tab.

Running the notebooks

The online tutorials display saved results. To change parameters and run the exercises, use Julia 1.12 or later and follow the installation guide. From the repository's pluto_tutorial folder, install the environment once, then launch Pluto and open the notebooks in order.

julia setup.jl
julia -t auto -e 'using Pluto; Pluto.run()'
