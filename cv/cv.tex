\cvsection{Profile}
\begingroup\fontsize{10.6}{13.4}\selectfont\raggedright
I study how household balance sheets shape consumption and
macroeconomic forecasts, the welfare effects of housing policy, and when estimated models can be trusted to evaluate policy,
including when microdata on households and firms improves their counterfactuals. I build
agent-based models with optimising households, calibrated to microdata and estimated with
simulation-based inference.
\par\endgroup

\cvsection{Research Interests}
\begingroup\fontsize{10.6}{13.4}\selectfont\raggedright
Macroeconomics, household balance sheets, inflation, housing and labour markets, agent-based and
heterogeneous-agent models, forecasting, expectations, misspecification, simulation-based inference.
\par\endgroup
