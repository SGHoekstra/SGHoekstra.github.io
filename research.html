Research

Papers & software

My work develops quantitative agent-based models of the macroeconomy and brings them to the data. I am particularly interested in combining the granular, stock-flow consistent structure of large-scale agent-based models with optimising, heterogeneous households, and in evaluating such models on their forecasting performance.

Finalist, Society for Computational Economics student paper contest (CEF 2025, Santiago)

Forecasting with a Dutch Agent-Based Model and Optimally Behaving Households

Steven Hoekstra

Job market paper · Latest version: September 2026

  • Paper (PDF)
  • Slides
  • Code
Abstract

This paper develops a large-scale agent-based model of the Dutch economy and evaluates its usefulness for macroeconomic forecasting. I extend the stock-flow consistent agent-based model of Poledna, Miess, Hommes and Rabitsch (2023) and calibrate it to Dutch micro and macro data. In place of rule-of-thumb consumption behaviour, households solve the two-asset consumption–savings problem of Kaplan, Moll and Violante (2018), which generates a realistic distribution of marginal propensities to consume across liquid and illiquid wealth. I compare the out-of-sample forecasting performance of the model against standard macroeconometric benchmarks and show how optimising household behaviour changes the model's predictions for aggregate consumption and its response to policy.

Software

  • BeforeIT.jl — contributor
    A high-performance Julia implementation of the macroeconomic agent-based model of Poledna et al. (2023), developed at Banca d'Italia. I use and contribute to the package for my own work on the Dutch economy.

    Calibration routines for BeforeIT.jl models, used to bring the model to national accounts and micro data.
