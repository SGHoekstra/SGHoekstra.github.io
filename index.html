Forecasting with a Dutch Agent-Based Model and Optimally Behaving Households

I extend the stock-flow consistent agent-based model of Poledna, Miess, Hommes and Rabitsch (2023) and calibrate it to the Dutch economy using micro data. Households solve the two-asset consumption–savings problem of Kaplan, Moll and Violante (2018), so that marginal propensities to consume vary across the wealth distribution. I evaluate the model in an out-of-sample forecasting exercise against standard macroeconometric benchmarks.
